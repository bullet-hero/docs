# Licensing: the whole thing in three minutes

Nothing is checked locally. All of this switches on at exactly one moment - when a level is handed to a service

> [!warning] Warning
> The services this section talks about - the official server and the site - do not exist yet. They arrive at release. Everything else here is already true

## Nothing is checked locally

Nothing is checked on a level sitting on your own disk. Not on create, not on save, not on load. A level made for yourself out of whatever files you have is your own business, and the editor imposes no conditions on it

Everything below switches on at exactly one moment: **when a level is offered to a service**

## The level's licence

Levels are distributed under `CC BY-NC`, which reads as:
- **CC** - Creative Commons
- **BY** - attribution required
- **NC** - non-commercial use only

That licence covers **your own work**: the layout, the timing, the keys, the composition. It does not relicense the external files inside the level. Every external resource keeps its own terms, recorded separately, per resource

> [!info] Worth knowing
> The game is free and monetised in no way: no purchases, no ads, no paid content, no donations tied to what is hosted. The non-commercial condition is therefore satisfied for the platform itself, and CC 4.0 licenses every downstream recipient directly from the author - so the chain author to server to player needs no additional grant

## Every resource has to qualify

A level qualifies only if every resource in it does. If even one satisfies neither of [[2_legal-resource-paths|the two options]], the level cannot go onto the official server

## What the licence does not cover

This is what service terms exist for: your assertion that you hold the rights you claim, the operator's right to moderate and remove, the right to show a level's name in listings, and the disclaimer of liability

The developers do not stand in the way of levels being shared freely. What they are categorically against is commercial use
