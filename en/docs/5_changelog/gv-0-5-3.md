# gv 0.5.3

A full-screen beat window, panels you can swipe and stretch, a reset button on every inspector field, and cleanup of old backups and statistics

- The beat window opens full screen with the `Open Beat window` button. It has one tapper and two results side by side: `Create from taps` makes a new beat segment, `Apply to selected` recomputes the tempo of the selected one. A recording can be listened to before it is applied, and the level keeps playing while the window is open
- `Time Format` in the editor settings chooses what the timeline rulers count in: frames, timecode, timecode with frames or `Bars & Beats`. The level itself does not change
- Editor panels open and close with a swipe on their handle, and an open panel can be stretched wider. Buttons got larger on phones
- Every inspector field has a `?` with a hint and a reset button, and every group has the same pair in its header. Resetting a group is one undo step
- `Link Collider To Shape`: while it is on, picking a shape also sets the matching collider, and the hitbox follows the silhouette
- A level can be deleted from the level list without opening it. The `Delete level` window is the same everywhere
- `Storage` in the settings deletes backups and statistics of levels that no longer exist, or all of them
- The viewport grid changes its cell size by exactly 10 times when zooming, like the scene grid in Unity itself
- Timelines draw most clips with one mesh instead of a separate interface element per clip, which is cheaper on long levels
- Effects and audio effects got explanations for their fields
