# gv 0.5.4

Vector graphics everywhere, health on the HUD, full colour curves, VSync, and Steam Workshop levels in the level list

- Every icon and interface image is now vector (SVG) instead of a texture
- The HUD shows lives as a row of dots above the progress bar. A lost life dims its dot instead of removing it, so the row keeps its length. With immortality the row is empty
- Colour curves became the eight curves of the URP engine: `Master`, `Red`, `Green`, `Blue`, `Hue Vs Hue`, `Hue Vs Sat`, `Sat Vs Sat` and `Lum Vs Sat`. Levels with the old two-value colour curves are converted on load
- Fixed: a colour curves keyframe with default values turned the picture grey and rotated its hues by half a turn
- The `VSync` setting with the values `Off`, `On` and `Half rate`, desktop only. While it is on, the framerate cap does nothing
- Steam version: levels you are subscribed to in the Steam Workshop appear in the level list. `Show All Found Content` also shows what is on disk but not confirmed as yours, such as an item without a subscription or a folder copied by hand. Such levels are marked `Not subscribed` or `Offline` on their card
- Every author of a level has a `Credit` field: music, cover, playtesting and so on
