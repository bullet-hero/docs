# Version 0.5.1

Checkpoints, statistics, new controls and a translated interface for players, a history tree and a beat grid for authors, Afterbeat import and export, and a 4x faster picture on phones

## Game

- Checkpoints work. A death rewinds the run to the last checkpoint it reached: the level clock eases to a stop over 0.5 seconds, jumps back and eases up again. `Open Menu on Lose` brings back the result window instead
- Statistics are kept on disk. The level screen shows `Best` and `Attempts` for the launch options you picked, and the `Profile` tab in the settings shows launches and time in the game, the menu and the editor
- Controls were rewritten for four devices: `Keyboard & Mouse`, `Touchscreen`, `Gamepad` and `Motion Sensor`. Touchscreens get on-screen controls
- Every keyboard shortcut can be rebound on the `Keybindings` tab, and the change applies to the whole game
- `Menu Background` offers `None`, `Shapes` and `Bot`. `Bot` is the new default: a live arena where a bot plays the avatar and dodges hazards in real time
- `Screen Orientation` setting and vertical layouts of the screens. A level declares its own orientation
- Built-in levels ship with the game. They play like any other level, cannot be edited, and can be copied into your own folder to edit the copy
- The interface is fully in English and Russian, with hints in the editor and on other screens
- Anti-aliasing: `MSAA` and `FXAA`

## Editor

- The `History` tab: undo history is a tree with branches, and you can jump to any point of it
- Timeline tools Trim and Blade, markers, and snapping to the beat grid
- The beat grid: beat segments, tapping along with the music on `T`, and `Create from taps` to turn the taps into a segment
- Selecting several objects and editing a field for all of them at once. Copy, paste and duplicate
- The raw data editor edits the whole level as data, for the cases the inspector does not cover
- Every picker is one search window with filters and tags
- One toggle shows the colliders of the whole level
- A `?` beside fields and panels opens a hint, and the editor settings carry a written guide for level authors
- `Autosave` works and writes backups into the `backups` folder of the game
- Editor preferences moved to the settings, into 9 sections of the editor tab

## Levels and format

- Shapes replace the default textures: about 500 shapes, generated by the game. The same shapes serve as colliders
- Afterbeat (formerly *Project Arrhythmia*) levels can be imported and exported
- The binary `.blob` format. The volcano test level (19 341 objects) takes 5.1 MB instead of 15.7 MB of JSON and reads in 203 ms instead of 9 946 ms. JSON itself reads 6 times faster: 1 837 ms instead of 11 249 ms

## Performance

- Phones draw levels about 4 times faster. On a Snapdragon 732G (Adreno 618) a frame of the stress scene went from 52.19 ms to 12.94 ms (77 fps)
- A loading screen between screens replaces the green screen that showed before
