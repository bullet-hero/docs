# gv 0.4.1

The level editor grows from a timeline and a hierarchy into a full editor, and levels get post-processing, audio effects and repeatable randomness

## Editor

- An inspector for objects, keyframes and level events. Before this version the editor had a timeline, a hierarchy and little else
- Five timelines: the level, the keyframes of one selected object, level events, audio and prefabs
- Gizmos in the viewport for position, rotation, scale, size, anchors and pivot. Each mode has its own keyboard shortcut
- Prefabs reworked: a template is edited in a separate Prefab Mode, and each placement can override the template's values
- Level resources are managed from the editor: audio, textures, fonts, colliders, effects, themes and prefabs. Effects, themes, colliders and prefabs can also be taken from ready-made libraries. File picking works only on desktop so far
- A custom collider editor, a curve editor, a gradient editor and ease presets with a preview
- An object can change its type and its parent without being recreated
- The selection is drawn as a marching dashed outline
- A level can be launched right from the editor. The avatar is visible in the editor, and post-processing can be switched off there
- The level settings got authors, licences, generators and rule validation, which finds problems in a level and offers to fix them

## Game

- Post-processing as level events, 12 in all: analog glitch, digital glitch, bloom, chromatic aberration, colour curves, film grain, lens distortion, lift gamma gain, motion blur, shadows midtones highlights, vignette and white balance
- Audio effects on audio tracks, 11 in all: chorus, compressor, distortion, echo, flange, highpass filter, lowpass filter, normalize, parametric EQ, pitch shifter and reverb
- Music sync rewritten. The game follows the audio clock and corrects a small drift with a slight change of pitch instead of a jump, so the audio stutter every few frames is gone
- Fixed: in the editor the timeline cursor slowed the level clock down by about 15%
- Randomness is repeatable: the same level with the same seed plays the same on any device. The level screen has a seed field with buttons to set a random one and to reset it
- The level screen shows the authors and licences of the level and of its resources

## Levels and format

- An object's lifetime is a start and a length. Each edge can be anchored to an edge of the parent, and then it follows that edge
- A layer counts relative to the parent instead of as an absolute number
