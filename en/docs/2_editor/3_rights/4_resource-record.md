# The resource record

The record notes where a resource comes from and on what terms: the link, the licence, permissions, authors. An empty licence field means "nobody checked", and such a resource is refused

> [!tip] Recommendation
> Fill in the record as soon as you add the resource. That is the only moment you still remember where it came from

## What the record holds

Every resource of yours in a level has its own record. It is stored inside the level and travels with it.
That way attribution is not lost: wherever the level ends up, the file carries where it came from and on what terms

The record's fields:
- **Title** and **description** of the original work, both can be translated into other languages
- **Url** - the work's page: the author's post or page, a store page
- **Licence** - the terms the work is distributed on
- **Permissions** - [[2_legal-resource-paths|option B]], one entry per rights holder, with scope, dates and evidence
- **Authors** - everyone who has to be credited for this resource, separate from the authors of the level itself
- **Sources** - free text about the origin, for whatever a link cannot express
- **Hashes** - the content hashes of the resource's files
- **AI generation** - whether the work was generated by AI: `Not specified`, `Not generated by AI` or `Generated by AI`

**The link points to the page with the terms, not to a direct download.** A moderator needs the page, not the file

## An empty field means "unknown"

> [!caution] Caution
> An empty licence field is `NoSpecifiedLicense`, and such a resource is refused. It does not mean "the terms are fine". It means "nobody checked"

The whole model works this way: an empty value means "nothing declared", not "everything allowed".
An age rating of `Unrated`, a permission scope of `Undefined` and an AI generation of `Not specified` work the same way

`NoSpecifiedLicense` can additionally state where the file was taken from: YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify.
The verdict does not change because of it. The moderator simply sees what conversation lies ahead

## Hashes

Fill in hashes from day one, even where they are not required.
A rights holder's complaint names a work. To answer it, every level with that work has to be found.
By name that is guesswork, by hash it is an exact search

## Stale records

A record of a resource the level no longer has gives advice, not an error.
It blocks nothing, but it is still worth removing

## A resource on the level card

A resource's record can be marked to show on the level card. This is meant for music.
A line with this resource then appears under the level's authors. Everything else stays in the full credits

## The cover's record

The level's cover has a record too. The cover is not a resource of the level, so its record is opened at the cover on the metadata tab, with the `Credits...` button

- A new image starts the record over, and it holds only the file's hash. A different image is a different work, and the previous authors would be credited wrongly
- The same file picked again leaves the record as it is
- Removing the cover removes its record too. Undo brings both back

A resource or a cover with `Generated by AI` makes the level screen show the line "Contains AI-generated content"
