# gv 0.11.1

Levels move as .zip too, the timelines got the Marquee tool and folding, and a dash depends on distance

There was no `gv 0.11.0`: the number went from `gv 0.10.1` straight to `gv 0.11.1`

- A level is exported and imported as `.zip`, not only as `.tar.gz`, including with a password. A `.7z` file is recognised and refused by name instead of being read wrong. A level "package" is now called an archive everywhere in the game (`Level Archive`)
- A dash to a point closer than the full range is a proportionally shorter dash: a shorter path, shorter invulnerability and a shorter cooldown. Closer than a third of the range there is no dash at all
- A dash without a direction does not happen. Before, it flew in an arbitrary direction you did not choose
- A dash is steered on every frame, and the invulnerability after a full dash is 0.30 s instead of 0.20 s. That equals the cooldown, so when you dash without a break you are exposed for exactly one frame between two dashes
- Timeline tools: `Selection`, `Edges`, `Scissors` and the new `Marquee` tool, which adds to the selection every clip touched by a dragged rectangle. `Edges` and `Scissors` used to be called Trim and Blade
- `Scissors` can cut a prefab placement in two, and both halves keep playing the same template from the point of the cut
- A subtree can be folded on the timeline: a double click on a clip with the `Selection` tool, a button in the tab row, the menu of a hierarchy row or `Ctrl+E`. Folding writes nothing into the level and creates no undo step
- Tooltips now appear in the released game, not only when it runs inside Unity. On a touchscreen a tooltip opens with a long press. The tooltip of an action with a keyboard shortcut shows that shortcut as it is currently bound
- More than 200 texts that were only in English are translated
