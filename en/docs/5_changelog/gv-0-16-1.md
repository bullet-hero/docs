# gv 0.16.1

The avatar walks at 15 units per second instead of 10, and the shortest dash equals one avatar body

- Walking speed is 15 units per second instead of 10. The avatar crosses the screen from top to bottom in two thirds of a second instead of one. When you steer with the cursor, the avatar catches up with the pointer at walking speed, so the lag after a sharp move became a third shorter. At 10 it felt slow in precise spots
- Dash and knockback stayed at 50 units per second, so both are now 3.3 times faster than walking instead of 5
- The shortest dash is one avatar body, 0.5 units. A target closer than that gets no dash at all. Without such a threshold, a cursor resting right next to the avatar triggered a dash every other frame, and each one had its own trail, sound and statistics entry

All the avatar's numbers are gathered on one page - [[6_avatar]]
