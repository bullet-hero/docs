# Version 0.5.4

Vector art everywhere, health on the HUD, full colour curves, VSync, and Steam Workshop levels in the level browser

- Every icon and interface image is vector (SVG) now instead of a texture
- The HUD shows your lives as a row of pips on top of the progress bar. A lost life dims its pip instead of removing it, so the row keeps its length. With immortality the row is empty
- Colour curves became the eight curves of the URP engine: `Master`, `Red`, `Green`, `Blue`, `Hue Vs Hue`, `Hue Vs Sat`, `Sat Vs Sat` and `Lum Vs Sat`. Levels with the old two-value colour curves are converted on load
- Fixed: a colour curves key with default values turned the picture grey and rotated its hues by half a turn
- `VSync` setting with `Off`, `On` and `Half rate`, desktop only. While it is on, the framerate cap does nothing
- Steam version: levels you are subscribed to in the Steam Workshop appear in the level browser. `Show All Found Content` also lists what is on disk but not confirmed as yours, such as an unsubscribed item or a folder copied by hand. Such levels are marked `Not subscribed` or `Offline` on their card
- Every author of a level has a `Credit` field for the role: music, cover art, playtesting and so on
