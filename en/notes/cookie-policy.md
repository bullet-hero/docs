# Cookie policy

This site stores two cookies and nothing else. There is no analytics, no tracking, no third-party scripts, and there is no profile of you anywhere

| Cookie | Value | Lifetime | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bh_consent` | `1` or `0` | 1 year | Records whether you accepted or declined cookies |
| `bh_lang` | `en`, `ru` or `zh` | 1 year | Remembers the language you selected |

`bh_lang` is written only after you accept cookies. If you decline, the site still works: your language is then determined by your browser's `Accept-Language` header on each visit and is not remembered between visits

Neither of these cookies can be read by anyone but this site, and neither of them leaves the server it is sent to
