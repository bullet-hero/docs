# Where to get resources

The trusted-source roster and its grades, and why YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify are named as refusals rather than simply left out

A licence field says what the author claims. The site it came from says how much that claim is worth. Together they are what makes moderation cheap

Every known site carries a grade:
- **Approved** - everything there is publishable, no per-resource check
- **Partially approved** - the site hosts more than one kind of terms, so the record is worth a glance
- **Needs a licence check** - every licence there is exists on that site, stated per upload
- **Needs a resource check** - terms depend on the work or on the plan its uploader chose
- **Not allowed** - nothing from here, whatever the record claims

**Audio, safe by default:** [ccMixter](https://ccmixter.org/) (all CC BY-NC), [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC), [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/) (CC BY), [Teknoaxe](https://teknoaxe.com/) (CC BY), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/) (CC0), [SoundImage](https://soundimage.org/), [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com). [GoodKid](https://goodkidofficial.com/creators/) declare nothing formally, but their FAQ amounts to CC BY without the attribution requirement. NCS works only while the level stays on the default `CC BY-NC`

**Audio, read the terms first:** [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/), [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/home), SoundBible, Playground

**Audio, questionable:** Zapsplat, [Play On Loop](https://www.playonloop.com/music-licensing/) - the latter only while the game is not a commercial project, which today it is not

**Images, safe by default:** [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/), [AmbientCG](https://ambientcg.com/), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/), [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/), [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/), [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)

**Images, read the terms first:** [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/), [Rawpixel](https://www.rawpixel.com/) (their Personal and Public Domain licences only), itch.io, Wikimedia Commons

**Fonts:** [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/)

> [!caution] Caution
> YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify are in the roster as **not allowed**. They are not a licence of any kind - they issue nothing to anyone. If a work exists only there, the honest record is "terms unknown", and the only route forward is Option B

> [!info] Worth knowing
> Naming them turns a moderation queue item into an immediate refusal at the moment the url is typed. An absent site grades as "somebody should look at this", which is far too mild for the most common way a level becomes unpublishable

> [!warning] Warning
> A site not being listed is not permission. It means nothing is known about it, and the roster is a starting point every operator may replace

> [!tip] Tip
> Matching is by host, subdomains included, from whatever url you typed. A bare address with no scheme still matches
