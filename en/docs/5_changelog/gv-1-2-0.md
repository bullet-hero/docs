# gv 1.2.0

Video export from the editor, profile transfer in one file, filters in the level list and your activity in Discord

## Video export

- `Export Video` in the side column of `Level Settings` renders the level into a video. The same command is in the command palette
- It needs ffmpeg `7.0` or newer installed on the computer, and the window finds it by itself. Windows, macOS and Linux, not on phones
- Frames are rendered one at a time, so the video is exact however slow the machine is
- MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, GIF, WebP, APNG, an image sequence or only the sound. 720p to 2160p or any size up to 7680, from 1 to 240 frames per second
- A bot plays the level (`Who plays`), and the grid, gizmos and selection never get into the frame

More - [[18_video-export]]

## Profile transfer

- `Settings` → `Other` → `Profile transfer` packs your profile into one zip file: levels, statistics, settings and the library. Autosaves, error reports and recordings go in if you tick them
- `Import` either replaces what you have (`Replace`) or adds to it (`Merge`). A merge keeps the newer copy of a level and the higher numbers of statistics
- What an import replaced stays as a backup, and `Restore` brings it back
- Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. Not available on iOS

More - [[14_profile-transfer]]

## Level list

- `Filters` opens a window with more filters: AI content, length, progress, recently played, age rating, levels with a password or from a newer version, and tags to require or to exclude
- `Reverse order` turns the sort around
- The search looks at level names first, then at authors, then at tags

More - [[2_playing-levels]]

## Discord

- The Steam builds show what you are doing in the game in your Discord profile: the menu, the editor, the sandbox or a level
- A level is named only when it is public: a Steam Workshop item or a level that ships with the game. Your own levels and the level you are editing are never named
- It works only while the Discord app is running on the same computer. You can hide it in Discord itself

More - [[game-privacy-policy#Discord Rich Presence]]

## Fixes

- Turning the sound off in the settings really silences levels. Before, it only greyed out the other sound settings
- A setting changed right before closing the game is no longer lost. A save icon appears each time the settings are written
- `Ctrl+S` saves the settings while they are open (`Save Settings`). In the editor it still saves the level
- Settings rows on phones no longer jump to their first option by themselves
- Some effects could disappear from the screen. Not any more
