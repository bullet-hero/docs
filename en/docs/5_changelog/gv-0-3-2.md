# gv 0.3.2

A small release for phones: twice as many objects on screen at once

The limit of objects drawn in one frame went up from 512 to 1024. About 1000 objects at once on a phone is the ceiling for now: more cannot be allocated in memory or drawn fast enough on a weak GPU

The shapes were also packed into one texture atlas, and a stress test scene was added to measure the limit
