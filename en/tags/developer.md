# Developer

A developer who builds a tool, a mod or a service on the open SDK, or extends the game itself

These pages describe the SDK: installing it, the level format and its binary `Blob` form, archives, versioning, validation, publish profiles, writing generators and the Afterbeat import. The SDK is open under MIT, so the source is the final reference
