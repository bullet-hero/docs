# Google Play testing

> [!info] Worth knowing
> This is temporary. Google Play publishes a new app only after it passes a closed test, so for now Bullet Hero is installed from there as a tester

The game in Google Play is the same full version as everywhere else, nothing is cut. Being a tester asks nothing of you: install it, open it, look around a bit, playing is not required

## Three steps

1. Join the group: [groups.google.com/g/bullethero-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/bullethero-testers)
2. Sign up as a tester **from your phone**: [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.vertoker.BulletHero](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.vertoker.BulletHero)
3. Install from Google Play: [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vertoker.BulletHero](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vertoker.BulletHero)

Without the first step the second one will not let you in, so keep the order
