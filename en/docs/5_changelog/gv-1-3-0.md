# gv 1.3.0

Achievements, avatar skins you draw yourself, seven new languages, Steam Cloud, and a difficulty and a performance rating on every level

## Achievements

- `Achievements` in the main menu lists 39 achievements, 4 of them secret
- They come from your statistics, so what you have already played counts from the first launch. An unlocked achievement never locks again
- Only other people's levels count, and only a fair run: no bot, collisions on, speed 1.00 or higher, lives on
- In the Steam builds they unlock on Steam as well. In the Google Play version they unlock in Google Play Games once you are signed in to it

More - [[15_achievements]]

## Avatar skins

- `Avatar` in the main menu opens the Avatar Hub. Pick a ready shape, change its colours, turn the eyes, hands, shadow, trail and other parts on or off
- `Editor` paints your own avatar on a grid of 3 to 8 cells a side
- A skin is one file. `Export` and `Import` in `My skins` move it, and profile transfer carries your skins (`Avatar skins`)
- A skin changes only the look. The hitbox and the speed are the same for every skin

More - [[16_skins]]

## Languages

- The game is now in Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Traditional Chinese, ten languages in all
- With `System` as the language in `Settings`, the game picks the language of your device

## Steam Cloud

- In the Steam builds, settings, statistics, achievements, levels, the library and skins follow your Steam account. There is no button: the game syncs at launch, while you play and on quit
- Graphics and controls stay each computer's own
- `Settings` → `Other` → `Cloud saves` shows whether it is on and when it last synced

More - [[14_profile-transfer]]

## Difficulty and performance

- A level has a difficulty from `Auto` to `Singularity`. The author sets it in `Level Settings` (`Difficulty`), and the level list and the level page show it
- Five bars on every level show how heavy it is for your device. `Performance on this device` in the level list filters by them
- Levels saved before this version show `Not measured` until their author saves them again
- `Settings` → `Graphics` → `Device Performance` → `Measure This Device` runs a stress test of up to 60 seconds. Until then the rating uses a typical device

More - [[2_playing-levels]], [[17_performance-rating]]

## Updates on Android

- In the Google Play and RuStore versions an update downloads in the background. When it is ready, the main menu shows `An update is ready`, and `Restart` installs it
- After a cleared level the game may ask you to rate it, not before your third cleared level and at most once in 60 days

More - [[1_installation]]

## AI assistant in the editor

- On a computer, an AI assistant that speaks MCP can edit the open level while you watch. It stays off until you turn on `Allow AI Connections` in `Settings` → `Game Editor` → `AI Agent (MCP)`
- Only this computer can connect. Every AI batch is one step in the history, and the AI cannot publish, delete or overwrite anything
- The AI keeps its notes in the level's `ai` folder. It goes with exports and Workshop uploads unless `Share AI context with this level` is cleared, and never with a password-protected export

More - [[7_ai-assistant]]

## Fixes and the rest

- The free builds for Windows, macOS and Linux come with `unins000`. It removes the game's own files and keeps your levels, settings and statistics
- After install the game reaches the menu faster, about 2 seconds sooner on a test phone
- Publishing checks that the level's license fits the licenses of its music
- In the editor a refused edit no longer leaves a broken step in the history, and the `Frame` field of a keyframe in the inspector moves the key
