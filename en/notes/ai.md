# Bullet Hero and AI

How I use AI while making the game, and what I think about levels made with it

I use AI, and I think it is worth using. But properly: as a tool whose work I check, not as something that thinks for me. Used without thought, it produces a lot of text and code that looks right and is not

## Where it helps

- **Code.** The game, this site and the SDK
- **Technical texts.** These docs and the translations, the Chinese version included
- **Ideas.** Discussing mechanics and design, looking for holes in a plan

Everything it produces I read, check and rework by hand. Nothing goes into the game or onto the site unread, and every line is my responsibility, not the model's

## Where it does not

The visuals. Everything you see in the game is made by hand, and I intend to keep it that way

## Levels made with AI

They are allowed. What matters to me is the quality of the level: if AI helps you make a level better, use it. Just say so in the level's description, so players know what they are playing

People who refuse AI on principle have my respect. A level made entirely by hand is a fine choice, and nobody is pushed off it
