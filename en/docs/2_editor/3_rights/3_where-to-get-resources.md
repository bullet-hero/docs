# Where to get resources

Safe by default: music from ccMixter, Freesound and Kenney, images from Poly Haven, Pexels and Unsplash, fonts from Google Fonts. Nothing may be taken from YouTube, SoundCloud or Spotify

## Where not to take from

> [!caution] Caution
> YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify are marked as **not allowed** in the roster. They are not a licence of any kind: they issue nothing to anyone. If a work exists only there, the honest record is "terms unknown". The only way forward is [[2_legal-resource-paths|the author's permission]]

## Audio

**Safe by default:**
- [ccMixter](https://ccmixter.org/) - CC BY, CC BY-NC
- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) - CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC
- [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/) - CC BY
- [Teknoaxe](https://teknoaxe.com/) - CC BY
- [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/) - CC0

**Worth a look at each record:**
- [SoundImage](https://soundimage.org/)
- [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)
- [GoodKid](https://goodkidofficial.com/creators/) - formally declare nothing, but their FAQ comes down to CC BY without mandatory attribution
- NCS - only while the level stays under the standard `CC BY-NC`

**Read the terms first:** [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/), [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/home), SoundBible

**Questionable:**
- Zapsplat
- [Play On Loop](https://www.playonloop.com/music-licensing/) - only while the game is not a commercial project. It is not commercial now

## Images and fonts

**Images, safe by default:** [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/), [AmbientCG](https://ambientcg.com/), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/), [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/), [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/)

**Images, worth a look at each record:** [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)

**Images, read the terms first:** [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/), [Rawpixel](https://www.rawpixel.com/) (only their Personal and Public Domain), itch.io, Wikimedia Commons

**Fonts:** [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/)

## Sites not in the roster

> [!warning] Warning
> A site missing from the list is not a permission. It means nothing is known about it

The roster itself is a starting point. Every service operator may replace it

## Grades

Every known site has a grade:

| Grade | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | everything from there can be published, no per-resource check needed |
| **Partially approved** | the site has more than one kind of terms, the record is worth a look |
| **Needs a licence check** | any licence can turn up there, and it is stated on each upload |
| **Needs a resource check** | the terms depend on the work or on the plan the uploader chose |
| **Not allowed** | nothing from here, whatever the record says |

The licence field says what the author claims. The site says how much that claim is worth.
Together they make moderation cheap

## How a site is recognised

A site is recognised by the host of the link you entered, subdomains included.
An address with no scheme (no `https://`) is recognised too

YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify are named in the roster directly, not simply left out.
That way a level with such a link is refused the moment you paste it. A site missing from the roster would only get "somebody should take a look". For the most common reason a level cannot be published, that is too mild
