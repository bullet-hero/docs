# Quick start

Six steps: from installing the game to your first finished and your first built level

Each step is the shortest path. Details are behind the links.
Short answers to common questions - [[6_faq]]

## 1. Get the game

Every build and store - [[download]]

## 2. Launch

You need three buttons of the main menu: `Levels`, `Editor` and `Settings`.
`Story` and `Multiplayer` do not work yet

## 3. Play your first level

Press `Levels`, pick a level and press `Play`.
You steer a small square. The goal is to survive to the end of the track

> [!tip] Tip
> For the first run, set `Lifes` to `Zen`. The run then never ends, and you can get used to the controls in peace

Someone sent you a level? It is a folder. Copy it into the `levels` folder and press `Scan again`.
More - [[2_playing-levels]]

## 4. Controls

| Device | Move | Dash |
|---|---|---|
| mouse | hold the left button | `Space`, `Shift` or the right button |
| touchscreen | drag a finger | a second finger |
| gamepad | either stick | the bottom face button or the right bumper |

Everything can be rebound in `Settings` → `Controls`.
More - [[3_controls]]

## 5. If something does not work

Common problems and fixes - [[5_troubleshooting]]

Where to report a problem - [[11_help]]

## 6. Build your first level

Press `Editor`, create a level and put a track (`ogg`, `mp3` or `wav`) into its folder.
A track of 1-2 minutes is enough to start

The editor saves the level by itself a minute after an edit. `Ctrl+S` saves right away

The whole route step by step - [[2_first-level]]
