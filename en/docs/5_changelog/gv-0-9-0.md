# gv 0.9.0

Health and the real hitbox are visible right on the avatar, and a level from a newer version opens with a warning

- The avatar's body is a grid of squares, and losing health knocks them out from the edge to the centre. A bad run shows on the player itself, not on a bar. `Shatter Effect` in the graphics settings turns this off: the body becomes one square that dims instead
- The ring of dots on the avatar lies exactly on its hitbox: radius 0.4 for a body 1 unit wide. The silhouette was never the hitbox, and now you can see where it really is. The same ring counts lives: a bright dot is a life you still have, a dim one is spent
- `Hitbox Ring Opacity` in the interface settings sets how visible the ring is, 0.6 by default. 0 turns it off, but levels are designed for a player who sees it
- A checkpoint got a `Restore Health` setting: the checkpoint refills your lives the moment the level passes it
- A level made in a newer version is marked `Newer version` in the level browser and opens after a warning. If something had to be replaced while reading it, the game writes a backup first and reports how many replacements there were
- An effect with `Loop` off emitted far fewer particles than with it on, and it looked as if the colour gradient had stopped working. A one-shot effect now emits as many particles as a looping one keeps alive
