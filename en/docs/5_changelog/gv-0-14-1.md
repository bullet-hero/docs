# gv 0.14.1

Fixes for phones - the screen stays on during a level, and memory cleanup no longer breaks a run - plus licence records and the return of the demo level

- The screen stays on while a level is playing, and obeys the system timeout again as soon as you pause. Before, a run where you steered with a held stick counted as inactivity, and the phone locked in the middle of a level
- A low-memory warning that arrives during a level is handled after the level stops. On Android the cleanup took 163 ms and 715 ms, and over 33 seconds of play it produced two frames of 735 ms and 523 ms, each of which was enough to lose a run. The cost: under a real memory shortage the system can now close the game during a level
- The game camera updates its output area only when it moves by a whole pixel. Before, this happened every frame, and the renderer recreated its intermediate images 60 times per second, even when the level's aspect ratio did not change
- The `Licenses` window folds: both levels of headers can now be expanded and collapsed. The level's own resources are open at once, and everything that ships with the game is folded
- Deleting a resource also deletes its licence record. Before, the `Licenses` window kept thanking the author for a resource the level no longer had. Undo brings the record back
- A level cover is saved as `logo.png` or `logo.jpg` according to what the image really is, whatever the source file was called. An image that is neither PNG nor JPEG is not accepted
- The built-in level `New zero demo` is back, with a cover and a credited music artist, Dunderpatrullen
