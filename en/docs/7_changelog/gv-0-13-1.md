# Version 0.13.1

Prefab placements move with their group, the History tab shows what each step changed, and the timeline tools sit on one block of keys

- Dragging a group that holds prefab placements now moves what those placements place. Before, the copies stayed on their old frames, and a move of one frame to the left was enough to print the error `Frame must be at least 1` on every frame for the rest of the session
- A trimmed placement keeps its own animation in place after you edit its span. Before, the edit slid the placement's keyframes against the content it places
- Every row of the `History` tab, and every undo or redo line in the console, now carries the numbers behind the step: the object's id and name, the track, and the frames it moved between (for example `f150 -> f160`)
- The timeline tools moved to one block of keys in the same order as the toolbar: `Z` folding, `X` snapping, `C` selection, `V` marquee, `B` scissors, `N` edges. Before, `V` was selection and `C` was scissors, so check your habits. A tab with fewer tools drops them from the right (the Local tab has `Z` `X` `C` `V`), so a letter never means a different tool on another tab. All six rebind in the settings, as `Tool: Snapping`, `Tool: Cycle Folding` and so on
- Validation no longer floods levels that use prefabs with false issues. On a level of 700 objects it reported 38 736 broken remaps and 19 993 dangling root overrides, and none of them were real
- The built-in level `New zero demo` is taken out of this version
